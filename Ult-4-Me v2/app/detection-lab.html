Recognition

Automatic uses masked structure for transparent images and V1 matching for opaque images. Masked structure compares visible light/dark patterns and tolerates brightness changes. Shape compares edges and silhouettes. V1 ignores alpha. Higher is stricter. V1 matching keeps the original threshold from Advanced settings. Scores are similarities, not probabilities. One is immediate. More checks reduce flicker but add delay and can miss brief events. Zero ends detection immediately. A brief hold can bridge animation gaps.
Saving updates V2 only. Applying restarts its detector; scoring and device settings are preserved.
